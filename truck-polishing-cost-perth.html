Why every truck-polishing quote is different

Truck metal does not arrive in one standard condition. A regularly maintained aluminium tank may need a straightforward polish, while a heavily oxidised bull bar can require cleaning, staged sanding, cutting and finishing. The size and shape of the component, access around fittings and the finish expected all affect the labour required.

Fleet Parlour assesses the actual metal before recommending work. This avoids quoting a light polish when restoration is required—or charging for restoration when a maintenance polish will achieve the required result.

The main cost factors

Condition and oxidation

Heavy oxidation, staining and old polish residue take longer to remove than light dullness.

Scratches and sanding

Deeper surface marks may require several sanding stages before polishing can begin.

Component size and access

Large tanks, complex bull bars, tight corners, brackets and fittings change the time required.

Finish required

A practical working-truck shine and a detailed mirror finish involve different preparation levels.

Services commonly quoted

Fleet Parlour provides job-specific quotations for bull bar polishing, aluminium fuel tank polishing, truck rim polishing, stainless-steel accessories and complete multi-component truck-polishing work. Fleet and repeat-work requirements can also be assessed.

How to get a more accurate quote

Send one photo showing the complete vehicle, clear close-ups of every component, and photos that show scratches or oxidation in natural light. Include the truck location, component count, preferred date and whether power and a covered work area are available. Fleet Parlour may recommend an inspection when photos cannot show the metal condition accurately.

Frequently asked questions

Can you quote from photos?

Yes. Good photos allow an initial assessment. The quote may need confirmation if hidden damage or deeper scratches become visible during preparation.

Is sanding always required?

No. Sanding is used only when the condition and required finish justify it. Sound metal may only need preparation and polishing.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes, for suitable locations across Perth, subject to access, power, weather protection and the requirements of the job.
