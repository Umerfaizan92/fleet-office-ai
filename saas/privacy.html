PRE-LAUNCH PRIVACY DRAFT

Privacy Notice

This privacy notice is a product draft for testing. Australian Privacy Act obligations, data locations, subprocessors and contact details must be legally reviewed before commercial launch.

What the workspace may hold

Depending on enabled modules, the service may hold account details, organisation settings, customer and enquiry records, job and booking information, workforce profiles, skills, qualifications, work-right information, uploaded documents, communications, invoices, payments, AI task history and audit events.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide the workspace, authenticate users, operate business workflows, generate user-requested drafts and recommendations, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and create audit records. Data should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

AI providers and integrations

Workspace data is not automatically sent to an external AI, telephony, messaging or social provider merely because its connection card appears in the interface. Data should be transmitted to a provider only after that integration is explicitly configured and enabled.

Organisation isolation and security

The application is designed around organisation-scoped records, authenticated sessions, rate limits, approval controls and optional MFA. No internet service can promise absolute security; production launch also requires persistent hosting, backups, monitoring, incident procedures and final security review.

Workforce information

Identity, work-right, employment and compliance records can be sensitive. Access should be limited according to role and business need, and retention periods should be defined before real employee records are stored.

Retention, access and deletion

Production policies for retention, export, correction, deletion and backup restoration will be published before commercial launch. The current Render test environment must not be treated as a permanent system of record.
