A receptionist that never puts anyone on hold

The AI receptionist add-on answers your business number, takes the details, offers times from your availability and texts you a summary. Bookings wait for your approval.

Ask about the add-on
Sample callHarbour Plumbing is an example business
  1. ReceptionistGood morning, you've reached Harbour Plumbing. This is the virtual receptionist. How can I help?
  2. CallerHi, the hot water system in our unit has stopped working.
  3. ReceptionistI'm sorry to hear that. Is any water leaking from the unit right now?
  4. CallerNo, it's just cold.
  5. ReceptionistThanks. Can I take your name and the suburb?
  6. CallerPriya, in Fremantle.
  7. ReceptionistThanks, Priya. I can offer tomorrow at 8am or 1pm. Which suits you?
  8. Caller8am please.
  9. ReceptionistI've requested 8am tomorrow. The team will confirm by text shortly. Is there anything else?
  10. In your workspaceEnquiry created, booking request waiting for the owner's approval, summary sent to the owner by text.

Receptionist questions

Is the receptionist included in every plan?

It is an add-on, priced by usage, and switched on for your business once your number is set up.

Does it make decisions for me?

No. It takes details and requests bookings. Bookings and replies wait for your approval.

Can callers reach me directly?

Yes. Genuine urgent calls can be transferred to you without your private number ever being shown.
