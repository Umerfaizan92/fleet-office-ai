Truck Polishing Questions

Which truck parts can you polish?

Common work includes bull bars, aluminium fuel tanks, rims and wheels, steps, checker plate, stainless-steel accessories and other suitable metal components.

Can you remove scratches and oxidation?

Many surface scratches, staining and oxidation marks can be improved through cleaning, staged preparation and polishing. The achievable finish depends on the metal and depth of the damage.

Do you provide mobile truck polishing across Perth?

Yes. Fleet Parlour travels to suitable sites across Perth, subject to safe access, power, weather protection and the scope of the work.
