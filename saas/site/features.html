What Super Pro does

Six parts of running a service business, sharing one set of records. Each one does the work and leaves the decisions to you.

Customers and work

Enquiries from calls, email and your website land in one place. Turn one into a quote, a booking and a job with the details carried across.

  • One record per customer
  • Quotes that follow up for you
  • Jobs your crew can run from a phone
  • Stock that knows where it went

Sending a quote, confirming a booking and merging customer records always wait for a person.

More about customers and work

Money

Invoices, payment links, tap-to-pay QR codes and receipts, with profit per job worked out as you go.

  • Pay links your customers trust
  • Tap to pay on site
  • Profit per job
  • Reminders without awkward calls

Sending an invoice, refunding, and changing payment settings are owner decisions.

More about money

Communications

Every conversation with a customer is in one place. Super Pro drafts the reply; you read it, change it if you like, and send it.

  • One inbox for every channel
  • An email centre with real control
  • Team chat that stays at work
  • The AI receptionist add-on

No message leaves the business until a person approves it.

More about communications

Content and growth

A growth plan for your business, a 30-day content calendar and posts written for each platform. You approve every one.

  • A plan made for your business
  • Posts for each platform
  • Search visibility, checked
  • Reuse what worked

Every post and every campaign waits for approval before it goes out.

More about content and growth

People and compliance

Worker records with work rights, licences and expiry dates, so you can see who is eligible for a job before you offer it.

  • Work rights recorded properly
  • Licences that remind you
  • Invite workers to onboard themselves
  • Permissions per person

Marking work rights as verified and changing permissions are owner decisions.

More about people and compliance

AI and approvals

Ask in plain words, by typing or speaking. Super Pro answers from your own records and prepares the work; you decide what happens.

  • Ask about your business
  • Drafts, not surprises
  • A record you can trust
  • Speaks your language

Anything that sends, pays, publishes or deletes needs a person to approve it.
