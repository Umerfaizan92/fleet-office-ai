COMPETITOR GAP RESEARCH — V14 SUMMARY

Current all-in-one CRM/marketing competitors such as HighLevel combine CRM/pipelines, unified conversations, email/SMS/calling, workflow automation, forms, calendars, social planning, reputation, payments, websites/funnels, mobile apps, courses/communities and AI tools. Current AI additions also include conversational agents, voice agents, AI website/funnel generation, logs and content/reputation automation.

V14 does not clone those products. The differentiation direction is:
- service-business operating workspace rather than marketing CRM alone;
- industry-adaptive AI setup for solo operators and teams;
- expense/job-profit intelligence integrated with operations;
- maker/checker quality-verification memory;
- governance, complaints, audit evidence and approval boundaries built into the product;
- customer/staff operational history and performance views;
- combined Content/Connections/Campaign metrics with no fabricated live data;
- secure operator-controlled provider keys;
- Answer Center + technical search/AI-discovery visibility controls.

Do not claim feature parity or superiority until each provider-dependent feature is connected and independently tested.
