PRE-LAUNCH LEGAL DRAFT

Terms of Service

This document is a product draft for testing and must be reviewed by a qualified Australian lawyer before commercial launch.

1. Trial and subscription

New workspaces receive a 14-day free trial. The plan selected at registration indicates the intended subscription after the trial. Current test-build billing is disabled, so no payment is collected until a payment provider is connected and the customer expressly authorises payment.

Indicative monthly prices are Starter A$99, Operations A$199 and Scale A$349, plus GST where applicable. Prices, limits and inclusions may be revised before commercial launch.

2. Usage-based services

Telephony, SMS, transcription, third-party AI processing and unusually high usage may later carry usage-based or add-on charges. Any such charges must be disclosed before they are enabled for a paying customer.

3. AI and human approval

AI features can draft, summarise, classify, recommend and prepare actions. AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and must be reviewed where the decision is consequential. Employment, legal, financial, compliance, payment and other material actions remain subject to authorised human review and approval.

4. Account security

Users are responsible for accurate registration information, safeguarding credentials and using the service lawfully. Multi-factor authentication should be enabled for privileged accounts. Attempts to bypass access controls, interfere with the service or access another organisation’s data are prohibited.

5. Customer and workforce data

The workspace owner is responsible for having a lawful basis to upload, use and communicate personal information relating to customers, workers and other people. Sensitive workforce and compliance records should only be collected where genuinely required.

6. Availability and integrations

External integrations depend on third-party providers, permissions, APIs and service availability. A provider shown in the interface is not considered connected until credentials and permissions are configured and a connection test succeeds.

7. Commercial launch

Refund rules, cancellation timing, service levels, governing law, dispute handling, liability limits and final company/contact information will be finalised as part of legal review before paid subscriptions are activated.
