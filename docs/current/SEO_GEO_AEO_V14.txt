SEO / GEO / AEO V14

Implemented:
- Public index title, description, canonical, crawl directives, OpenGraph/Twitter metadata and SoftwareApplication/Organization/WebSite JSON-LD.
- Public robots.txt and sitemap.xml generated by server. Private workspace, auth, Office Manager and APIs are excluded from public crawling.
- Answer Center with concise capability/security answers written for humans and machine retrieval.
- IndexNow readiness and admin submission endpoint.
- Search Visibility Centre to inspect technical readiness.
- llms.txt is included only as an optional machine-readable summary. It is NOT treated as a Google ranking signal.
- No FAQPage rich-result dependency; the product uses semantic headings and direct answers instead.

CURRENT 2026 SEARCH NOTES USED FOR THIS BUILD
- Google Search Central (June 15, 2026) clarifies llms.txt is not needed for Google Search and does not positively or negatively affect Google visibility/rankings. Therefore v14 treats llms.txt as optional, not a ranking tactic.
- Google removed FAQ rich-result documentation in June 2026 because FAQ rich results are no longer shown. Therefore v14 does not rely on FAQPage rich-result markup.
- Google supports SoftwareApplication structured data and recommends validating structured data, keeping pages crawlable and submitting a sitemap.
- Bing Webmaster Tools added AI Performance reporting in 2026 for citations/grounding in supported AI answer experiences. V14 includes a place to record Bing AI visibility snapshots.
- IndexNow is used only to notify participating search engines about added/updated/deleted public URLs. It is paired with a sitemap, not treated as a ranking guarantee.
