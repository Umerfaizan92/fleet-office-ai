Pricing questions

Is GST included?

No. Prices are in AUD and GST is added on your invoice.

What happens after the trial?

Choose a plan to keep going. Your records are kept either way.

What are usage add-on packs?

Extra storage, AI requests and similar usage you can add when you need it. Operations saves 25% and Scale saves 50% on packs.

Is the AI receptionist included?

It is an add-on, priced by usage and switched on once your number is set up.
