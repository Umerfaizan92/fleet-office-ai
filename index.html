Professional Truck & Metal Polishing Services in Perth

Fleet Parlour provides premium mobile truck and metal polishing services across Perth, Western Australia.

We specialise in bull bar polishing, fuel tank restoration, alloy rim polishing, stainless-steel polishing and aluminium restoration. Using professional polishing techniques, we transform dull, oxidised and weathered metal surfaces into a premium mirror finish.

From owner-operator trucks to commercial fleets, we deliver exceptional workmanship, attention to detail and lasting results that keep your vehicles looking their absolute best on the road. Learn more about our truck polishing service in Perth.
