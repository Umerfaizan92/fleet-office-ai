# Compact layouts and live staff presence

- Removed the visible Motion on/off switch. Decorative animation remains automatic, bounded to visible icons and paused for reduced-motion/Data Saver preferences and hidden pages.
- Removed viewport-height stretching from the public hero, guide shell, short workspace pages and their footers. The chat composer and controls remain visible while conversation history scrolls. Quick prompts use a compact horizontal rail. Newsletter, pricing, trial and subscriber surfaces use tighter spacing; newsletter consent and unsubscribe remain explicit.
- Native dialogs use fit-content height with a viewport maximum. This fixes the Terms prompt stretching between its top/bottom insets. Consent remains mandatory and the acceptance action stays disabled until checked. Other dialogs share compact padding, while long forms can scroll rather than hide controls.
- Availability no longer implies online presence. Explicit browser-view heartbeats are bound to the authenticated business, user and session. Default roster contains only recent live views on active accounts. Sign-out deletes the session and cascades its presence rows. Hidden/closed views send a leave beacon; interrupted connections expire after 90 seconds. Another genuinely live tab/device keeps its person online. Offline staff are collapsed separately for authorised managers; nobody is deleted from the business.

Validation uses throwaway accounts: responsive landing/plan/newsletter layouts and composer visibility at 390, 980 and 1440 pixels (including a tall phone in desktop mode); short dashboard footer spacing; content-sized Terms dialogs with mandatory consent; both portals' navigation; cross-session chat/logout roster updates. Runtime tests cover saved-availability false positives, stale heartbeats, separate live tabs, sign-out cleanup and tenant restrictions. No production account or staff record was edited. Render deployment remains manual.
